_(coaxing)_ Uh-huh.

_(reluctantly)_ I've... not been sleeping well lately. 

#### GEORGIE

Brilliant! I mean, well, not for you. But it does tie in well with our latest sponsor.

##### [FX: short tacky jingle to indicate the start of an ad]

#### GEORGIE

_(groans unconvincingly)_ Man, I need coffee! 

#### CELIA

_(flat, actually tired)_ Were you up all night researching conspiracy theories? 

#### GEORGIE

No, I just can't fall asleep! 

#### CELIA

You poor thing. You should treat yourself to a SleepTight mattress. 

#### GEORGIE

A SleepTight mattress? Why? 

#### CELIA

Because a SleepTight mattress uses state of the art foam technology and over 333 micro-springs to shape and set itself around your body, holding you in place all night long. 

#### GEORGIE

Sounds too good to be true!

Then you should take the SleepTight 90 Night Challenge: try it for 90 nights, and if you're disappointed, you can apply for get 10% back! 

#### GEORGIE

Wow, they must really have faith in their product! That's my worries thoroughly debunked. I'm going to get a SleepTight mattress, right now!

##### [FX: sleepy tinklies fade out.]

#### CELIA

It's your turn to pick the topic, so: which conspiracy are we unpicking today? 

#### GEORGIE

Today, we're *jetting* off to the 90s on the *trail* of a real --

#### CELIA

Is it chemtrails? 

#### GEORGIE

Yes, it's chemtrails: the claim that those clouds you see coming out the back of planes are full of chemicals being purposefully spread by secretive organisations for... reasons.

##### [FX: excessively spooky sting] 

#### CELIA

OK. So why would anyone think there's something sinister going on? 

#### GEORGIE

Well, let's start by talking about the difference between "chemtrails" and "contrails". So, contrails are just frozen condensation following in the plane's wake after cold air combusts in a jet engine --

#### CELIA

Great, well, that seems pretty straightforward. Good episode! Tune in next week when --

#### GEORGIE

Ah! But while *chemtrail* believers do accept that contrails are a thing, they claim that chemtrails linger in place for much longer than contrails. 

#### CELIA

Right. And that's convincing because, of course, clouds never last more than 5 minutes. 

#### GEORGIE

Yeah, so like clouds, contrails behave differently in different conditions. Stuff like temperature, altitude, humidity: it all affects how -- and even *if* -- condensation forms and disperses. But some people claim that the thick type are always followed by weird effects. 

#### CELIA

Like what? 

#### GEORGIE

Mm. Rain, mostly. 

#### CELIA

Right. And we're sure these people are seeing chemtrails and not just, you know... clouds? 

#### GEORGIE

Well, there's loads of pictures online and some of the photos do have a *lot* of contrails in them, like you'd have to live on some sort of aviatory ley line to have that many flight paths criss-crossing over your head, and this is assuming you can even see whatever chemicals are said to be spread... 

#### CELIA

If chemtrails come down to "it's theoretically possible something invisible exists somewhere," then we may as well be asking if Santa's sleigh is spreading asbestos or something! What do believers think chemtrails are actually *for?* Who's supposed to be spreading them? And why? 

#### GEORGIE

Well, there are loads of competing theories, but most people agree that chemtrails are either for: mind control, weather manipulation, biological warfare, weapons testing, population control, or combatting climate change. 

#### CELIA

Well, that certainly narrows it down. 

#### GEORGIE

Yeah, so let's start with the mind control theory, because it's probably the easiest to dismiss. 

#### CELIA

Well, you would say that, given they're controlling your mind. 

#### GEORGIE

The idea is that governments are spreading vaguely defined "mind control chemicals" to make the population more docile. 

#### CELIA

And people think it works? 

#### GEORGIE

Well, yes. Their theory is that only mind-controlling substances could get people to not notice the mind-controlling substances in the sky. 

#### CELIA

I see. 

#### GEORGIE

Yeah, it's just not realistic. It would be *so* inefficient.

#### CELIA

...What? 

#### GEORGIE

I'm just saying it would be a terrible way of spreading mind control chemicals. It's so wasteful and you don't have any way to properly monitor the effects... I just think there are better options. 

#### CELIA

So how would you do it? 

#### GEORGIE

Oh, water supply. Easy. Almost everyone uses tap water and it would be simple to add a few extra chemicals to reservoirs during disinfection. You could even target specific regions. And it would be greener than using all those planes. 

#### CELIA

Oh, well, I'm so glad to hear you've considered the environmental impact. 

#### GEORGIE

Trouble is, there aren't really any "mind control chemicals" that work like that. Even the CIA gave up after the whole MK-Ultra thing. Best they managed was *maybe* making a couple of people a *bit* more suggestible. 

#### CELIA

So I can keep drinking tap water? 

#### GEORGIE

Besides, you don't need chemicals to control people. That's what the economy's for! 

#### CELIA

Plus, every social media platform's already basically a mind control device already. 

#### GEORGIE

Very true. 

#### CELIA

Georgie, you do realise you're on all of them, all the time right? 

#### GEORGIE

Right, but I interact with random stuff to confuse the algorithm. My targeted ads think I'm a French grandpa who likes windsurfing and motorbike repair.

Anyway, moving onto the weather manipulation stuff -- this is where the conspiracy started. In 1996, the US Air Force put out a report entitled "Owning the Weather in 2025," which looked at how the US government could manipulate weather in the future and --

#### CELIA

-- and is, to be fair, something a supervillain's research assistant would write. 

#### GEORGIE

Yeah, nobody really noticed it 'til 1999 when Art Bell, who hosted *Coast to Coast AM,* connected the dots with online forums discussing contrails and then suddenly the theory was everywhere. Apparently, the US government got *so many* complaints about it. 

#### CELIA

That must have been such a weird day to be on the US Air Force customer service hotline. Everyone accusing you of ruining Brad Washingtonson's picnic or whatever. 

#### GEORGIE

Well, most of the more "serious" posters think it's either to combat -- or even cause -- climate change, depending on which one they think is worse. 

#### CELIA

Well that's a bit of a stretch --

#### GEORGIE

_(incredulous)_ But governments *can* use aircraft to affect the climate. And they do! 

#### CELIA

_(sceptical)_ I'm sorry, governments can control the weather? 

#### GEORGIE

A bit, yes! In 2008, the Chinese government wanted to make sure Beijing was nice and sunny for the Olympics, so they used "cloud seeding" to purposefully make it rain in a different city, and kind of...wring all the moisture out of the air before it got to the sports. 

#### CELIA

Cloud seeding? 

#### GEORGIE

It's when you release chemicals -- usually some kind of salt -- into the air at high altitudes to cause ice crystals to form at higher temperatures than normal and make clouds which then fall as rain, or hail, or snow, or whatever. 

#### CELIA

Okay, even I can see how you could mix that up with chemtrails. 

#### GEORGIE

Well, if you like cloud seeding, you'll love this: some climate scientists are suggesting that we *could* use contrails to help mitigate global warming. 

#### CELIA

I'm sorry, what? 

#### GEORGIE

Well basically, because contrails are essentially just clouds, if you get enough of them in one spot, they should have an insulating effect. So, you could, in theory, control heat build-up and dispersal around the planet. 

#### CELIA

Okay, so planes could *hypothetically* be used for some minor weather shenanigans. That hardly seems as bad as the whole mind-control thing. 

#### GEORGIE

Well, you say that, but silver iodide is one of the chemicals used for cloud seeding, and in extreme quantities, it can be carcinogenic. And turn your skin a bluey-grey colour. 

#### CELIA

I'll admit cancer rain doesn't sound great. So have there been many unexplained cases of silvery skin? 

#### GEORGIE

No. The amounts used for cloud seeding are super tiny. 

#### CELIA

Right. So the weather stuff is A) fine, and B) not what people are talking about when they're talking about chemtrails. 

#### GEORGIE

Pretty much. 

#### CELIA

So the mind control's nonsense, and the weather control stuff is doesn't scan either. Sound like this is pretty much done? 

#### GEORGIE

Not quite. We still have one last stop on our flight path, but we'll get to it after a flying visit from our sponsor...

##### [SFX: Buh-Dum-Tss] 

#### CELIA

Do we really need all these puns? 

#### GEORGIE

Yes. Yes we do.

##### [SFX: A gavel bangs, a bassy jazzy tune plays]

#### GEORGIE

Gosh darn it, this well-known online auction site is so hard to use! I just want to make some quick cash, but they won't let me sell this junk! 

#### CELIA

Most e-commerce sites have ridiculous rules about what can and can't be sold, but the folks over at SoulTrader believe in the free market. That's why they've created the world's first dedicated online soul-selling marketplace.

##### [SFX: Ghostly Gasps]

#### CELIA

SoulTrader: because a Faustian bargain is still a bargain!

##### [SFX: cha-ching] 

#### CELIA

_(aside)_ Where do you find these sponsors? 

#### GEORGIE

_(aside)_ There's a directory. The weirder ones pay more...

_(back "on")_ Anyway! We were about to cover the weirder side of airborne chemicals, including our favourite: "messed up cases of verified chemical warfare!" 

#### CELIA

I'm guessing you're talking stuff like Agent Orange? 

#### GEORGIE

It was a chemical agent spread by aircraft... 

#### CELIA

Sure, but commercial flights are completely different. For chemtrails to be a thing, you'd have to believe that pretty much *every* government and airline is working together on this. And no government is going to just let someone spray their citizens with --

#### GEORGIE

But they do! They do it themselves! 

#### CELIA

What do you mean they do it themselves? 

#### GEORGIE

The MoD has already admitted to doing this loads of times! In the Fluorescent Particle Trials, planes flew across the length of England, dropping zinc cadmium sulphide, and in the Large Area Coverage Trials, MoD ships sprayed *E. coli* all over Dorset. In the Sabotage Trials, they released a bunch of bacteria on the Northern Line --

#### CELIA

Wait, what?! 

#### GEORGIE

And this is just from *one* military base: Porton Down. They admitted it all in a report they gave to this Lib Dem MP, Norman Baker, way back in 2002.

#### CELIA

They're not still doing it, though. Are they? 

#### GEORGIE

Let's just say that when Porton Down was asked about that, they said "it's not our policy to comment on current research." So... yeah, it certainly sounds like there's some current research to not comment on. 

#### CELIA

Well, that's horrifying. And not in a fun conspiracy kind of way. 

#### GEORGIE

Exactly. So I'm not saying chemtrails are real, I'm just saying: someone, somewhere, is definitely getting sprayed! And it could be you! It could be any of us! 

#### CELIA

And there's nothing we can do about it? 

#### GEORGIE

Sure there is! Just try not to breathe. Or drink water. Or eat anything that grows in the ground! You know, basic stuff. 

#### CELIA

_(laughs)_ Uh-huh. Well, despite these awful revelations, none of that stuff is actual "chemtrails," is it? 

#### GEORGIE

No. And there's really no evidence that commercial jets are deliberately spreading stuff. 

#### CELIA

Just classified military experiments on the civilian population? 

#### GEROGIE

Exactly! 

#### CELIA

Okay. Well, I have to say I think you've convinced me. 

#### GEORGIE

Really?! 

#### CELIA

Not about chemtrails, which are clearly utter bollocks, but the idea that militaries are dumping chemicals on people from the air for sinister reasons? Absolutely. 

#### GEORGIE

I'll take that as a win! Wow! I'm gonna award myself a certificate to commemorate this momentous achievement. 

#### CELIA

Do I get a certificate for every time I've convinced you a theory's fake? 

#### GEORGIE

Steady on. Printer ink's expensive, you know. 

#### CELIA

Damn. 

#### GEORGIE

Best I can manage is the drone I've sent to spray caffeine through your air vents to perk you up before recordings. 

#### CELIA

Sounds great. 

#### GEORGIE

So got anything good planned this weekend? 

#### CELIA

This and that. But more importantly, are you excited about your big date? 

#### GEORGIE

Not particularly. I've been checking online and the odds of him being a serial killer are shockingly high. 

#### CELIA

Even if he is, I doubt he's going to kill you in a Zizzi's. 

#### GEORGIE

He might!

Why do I feel like you're still holding out for a certain famous YouTube ghosthunter? 

#### GEORGIE

I'm allowed to admire good videography. 

#### CELIA

Especially when it's by Melanie King, right?

I'm just saying she has soulful eyes, she looks like she's seen terrible things. In a good way.
